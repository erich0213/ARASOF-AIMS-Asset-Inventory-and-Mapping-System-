CAMPUS ASSET MAPPING SYSTEM - DIAGRAMS & SYSTEM DESIGN

1. ENTITY RELATIONSHIP (ERD) STRUCTURE
-----------------------------------------------------
- CollegeDepartment (1) ------ (N) CampusAsset
  > DepartmentID (Primary Key)
  > DepartmentName, DepartmentCode, Custodian

- AssetCategory (1) --------- (N) CampusAsset
  > CategoryID (Primary Key)
  >  CategoryName, Description

- CampusBuilding (1) -------- (N) FacilityRoom
  > BuildingID (Primary Key)
  > BuildingName, BuildingCode, TotalFloors

- CampusBuilding (1) -------- (1) GISCoordinate
  > GISID (Primary Key)
  > Latitude, Longitude, MapPinLabel, ZoneArea

- FacilityRoom (1) ---------- (N) CampusAsset
  > RoomID (Primary Key)
  > RoomNumber, RoomType, FloorLevel
